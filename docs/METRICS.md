# Metrics to Track

## 1. Acquisition Metrics
- **Traffic Sources**: Where are auditors coming from?
- **Landing Page Conversion**: CTA click-through rate.

## 2. Product Engagement
- **Audit Completion Rate**: Percentage of users who finish the form.
- **Average Tools Added**: Insight into the complexity of modern stacks.
- **Total Savings Identified**: Aggregate value provided by the platform.

## 3. Viral Metrics
- **Share Link Generation**: How many users click "Share Results"?
- **Referral Traffic**: Traffic coming from `results/[id]` pages.

## 4. Business Metrics
- **Lead Capture Rate**: Percentage of auditors who provide their email.
- **Credex Click-throughs**: Percentage of high-savings users who click "Talk to Credex".
