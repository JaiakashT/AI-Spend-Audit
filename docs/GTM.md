# Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

## Launch Plan
1. **Product Hunt**: Launch on a Tuesday at 12:01 AM PT.
2. **Twitter/X**: Tag AI influencers and "Build in Public" community.
3. **LinkedIn**: Target CTOs and CFOs with "Burn Reduction" messaging.

## Content Strategy
- **Case Studies**: "How Startup X saved $4,000/year using this audit."
- **Infographics**: Comparison of AI coding tool pricing (Cursor vs Copilot vs Windsurf).

## Virality Loops
- **Shareable Links**: Every audit generates a public URL that looks great in Twitter/Slack previews.
- **Team Reports**: Encourage users to "Email this report to my CEO."

## Key Messaging
- "Stop the AI burn."
- "The free audit that pays for itself in 60 seconds."
- "Your AI stack is overlapping. Fix it."
