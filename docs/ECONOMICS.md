# Unit Economics & Monetization

## Target Audience
- Pre-seed to Series B startups.
- Engineering managers and CTOs looking to optimize burn.

## Value Proposition
The average startup wastes ~30% on AI subscriptions. For a 10-person team, this can be $500+/month.

## Monetization Potential
1. **Lead Generation**: Feed qualified leads to "Credex" (the parent brand) for high-ticket enterprise AI procurement.
2. **Referral Fees**: Partner with AI vendors (e.g., Cursor, OpenAI) for plan upgrades or specialized credit packages.
3. **Bulk Credits**: Selling discounted API credits directly via the platform.

## Cost to Run
- **Hosting**: $0 (Vercel Free/Pro)
- **Database**: $0 (Supabase Free Tier)
- **AI Summary**: ~$0.01 per audit (GPT-4o-mini)
- **Email**: $0 (Resend Free Tier)

**Gross Margin**: ~99% per audit.
